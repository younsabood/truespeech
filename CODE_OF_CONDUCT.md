# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful in issues, reviews, and other project discussions.
- Discuss technical ideas directly without attacking people.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and abusive or sexualized conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when these expectations are violated. To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers through the contact details on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not publish another person's private information. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) only for vulnerability reports.
